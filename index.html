Smelter Yard

Fight through the yard, climb the furnace shaft, time the crusher hall, ride the lava conveyor and defeat the Forge Warden. Expect a first run to take a few minutes.

Spikes and the lava take a quarter of your health and put you back at the last checkpoint. Running out of health or getting crushed costs a life; you get 3 per run. Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims, A jumps, B or RB dashes, RT or X shoots, Start pauses.

The Test Yard is the short practice course from earlier milestones. Start captures the mouse; Esc releases it. If your browser won't capture it, hold the left button and drag to look, and shoot with F.
